CW raised that SU were seeking to transfer ownership and running of the RAP Drop Ins to NHS-OA, with

### Conference
It was queried whether there will be a conference this year.
CW reported that it is unlikely to be an in person one. 
An online conference or 'event week' or similar could be still possible but given internal SU
review of conferences that is ongoing, this is not certain.
We discussed supporting more informal small-group in person events e.g. short lectures or 
just social gatherings. CW said that other groups already do this regionally - perhaps we can let the 
community know about these, or seek to fill gaps where they may exist between existing offers?
All agreed to seek what is available in their area and report back to next committee meeting on this.
