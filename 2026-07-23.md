SC and CW to meet offline to discuss further.

### Minimal suppoprt plan
Given financial and considerable time constraints, CW reported that the SU team require to reduce our NHS-OA related workload, but that we are keen to maintain the value in the community.
CW presented a plan for a reduced scope of activity that included more infrequent meetings, fewer workshops and webinars, ceasing to provide the newsletter, and no further conferences as standard.
All were asked for their feedback on this plan.
All agreed it was proportionate and sensible.
JP raised that how people are learning to code is different with the advent of AI support.
We discussed how this may have affected the value we offer, and may be reflected in the change to how the community is engaging with us.
It was suggested that, if we cannot hold our own conference, it would be beneficial for us to have a presence at other peoples' conferences, provided we can be clear on 
our value and what we offer.
SC offered to allow NHS-OA to 'piggy back' on the NHSE DS newsletter if we had occasional things we'd like to post, since the audiences are similar.
DW posed the question of what change in the NHS OA wanted to see.
SR said the HSMA community was facing similar questions, and had recently started investigating Discourse as a Slack alternative, to ameliorate the data persistence issue.
SR also offered her assistance if we wanted to do online conferences in future.
JW suggested holding online unconferences instead - CS and CW reported that this is already part of the plan, as provided by Pawel and Brittny from Edinburgh Uni.
MF offered to link us up with contacts who may be willing to fund community activities.
CW thanked him, and said we would follow up, but that it would be beneficial for us to get to grips with whe the community stands for before asking for funding.
