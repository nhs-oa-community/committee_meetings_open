## Topics Covered

### Welcome to Pawel Orzechowski
PO, Lecturer in Programming for Data Science at the University of Edinburgh, joins us today as a new starter with the committee. 

### Merger update
The merger work is largely complete. 
We hope to complete the outstanding elements of the NHS-OA website and NHS-OA Way book this week and will revisit how we can minimise duplication in content between them post-deployment.
The NHS-R website will remain live to host older blog posts but will be stripped of any conflicting events information.
We now have one unified LinkedIn presence.
The python community are still not being served as well as the R community by NHS-OA.
Discussion was had around whether the NHS-OA website should host links to external packages and research projects showcasing health care data science.
SR posited that these links would be best retained in the [Atlas](https://atlas.hsma.co.uk/about/) which could be linked to from the OA website.

### Training
The Introduction to R and Intermediate R training materials are still on the NHS-R GitHub organisation.
These need to be re-sited onto the NHS-OA organisation, with care to be taken that all external links are updated and the move does not disrupt any impending training cohorts.
SWM, CS and MF to discuss how to manage this move.
DW is in progress producing an Introduction to Python course and hopes to feed back on progress soon.

### Newsletter
The NHSE Data Science Substack newsletter has agreed to host NHS-OA news and content when necessary - thank you! 
We will communicate the cessation of the NHS-OA Substack newsletter in due course.

### Unconference
PO has kindly offered to organise, staff and host an online unconference event for us in late November.
This half day event will be hosted on the University of Edinburgh Teams platform, supported with Slack for communications and a shared Google doc (or similar) for the timetable.
Interested parties will let CW know via email if they wish to assist in organising/promoting this event.
Committee members are politely requested to attend as senior discussion leaders.
Should this event be a success, we will consider holding semi-frequent lunchtime version on an ongoing basis.

### Vision
The committee had started to discuss how our community vision should be revised in light of the merger and the changing nature of NHS analytics and data science.
CW agreed to draft a new vision and send round for comment.
The vision, once accepted, would be used as a basis of conversations on what we need to provide in future, and will be hosted on the NHS-OA Way book.




### Next meeting
Our next committee meeting will likely be in December.
A set of options will be sent out shortly.
